# The Deck

A 52-card bodyweight workout game you can save to your phone's home screen.

- Five suits (Upper Body ▲, Core ●, Lower Body ◆, Arms ✚, Legs ■), cards 1-10 by difficulty, plus 2 wildcards.
- Take the weekly baseline test, then every card's reps are worked out from your baseline.
- Shuffle, flip, do the card, move on. Points, streaks, runs, skips and the clean-deck bonus follow the rulebook.

Your baselines and workout history are saved on your phone only.

## Files
- `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`: the app
- `data.js`: every card, its percentage of baseline, and how its reps are shown
- `cards/`: card pictures (the rep number is filled in by the app)
- `sw.js`, `manifest.json`: let it work offline and install to the home screen
